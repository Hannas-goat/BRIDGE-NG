Job board sync

Internal tool — pull live listings from a company's public Greenhouse or Lever job board straight into Bridge NG. Synced jobs are saved to the platform and trigger alerts for any matching saved search or followed company. Requires the server's import token.

The slug is the company identifier in their board URL — e.g. boards.greenhouse.io/figma or jobs.lever.co/ro.

Browse this sync

These roles are already live on Bridge NG and matched against real signed-in users' skills on the Job seeker tab — this is just a quick way to review what came in from this sync.
