About Bridge NG

Closing the gap between graduating and getting hired.

What Bridge NG is

Bridge NG matches Nigerian students and early- to mid-career professionals to real roles by actual skill overlap — not just keywords on a CV — and gives employers a shortlist that's already relevant. Job listings come from verified public job boards (Greenhouse and Lever) for real companies, plus roles employers post directly on the platform. Matching, resume tailoring, and cover letters are handled by AI, but every job link and every application goes to a real posting.

Who's behind it

Bridge NG is built and operated independently by one person — not a large company with a dedicated support team. That means changes ship fast, but it also means if something's broken or missing, the fastest way to get it fixed is to say so directly.

Why it exists

The gap between finishing school and landing a first real role is one of the hardest parts of a career in Nigeria — made harder by scattered listings, generic advice, and applications that go nowhere. Bridge NG exists to close that specific gap: real jobs, matched on real skills, with tools (resume tailoring, interview prep, training resources) built around the parts of the job search that are hardest to get right alone.
