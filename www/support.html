Support

Common questions, answered directly.

How does job matching work?

Your match score is based on real overlap between your listed skills and a role's required skills, plus your career stage and preferred location. Set your location to "Any location" at signup or in your profile if you're not sure yet — you'll see roles from every state and every remote-friendly listing, unfiltered.

I forgot my password — how do I reset it?

There's no automatic password-reset flow yet. Email us from the address on your account (or with enough detail to verify it's really you) and we'll help you sort it out directly.

How do I apply to a role?

Sign in, then use the "Apply" button on any listing. If the role has a real posting link, you're taken straight to it and the application is saved to your account under "Your applications" so you can find it again.

How does resume tailoring work?

In Resume Studio, upload or paste your CV, then paste the job you're applying for (the fuller the posting, the better the result). Bridge AI rewrites your resume to emphasize what's already true and relevant — it never invents experience, employers, or dates. You can download the result as a Word doc, PDF, or plain text.

I'm an employer — how do I post a role?

Sign in and go to the Employer tab. Posting a role runs it against real candidate profiles immediately and shows you a ranked shortlist by match score.

Something looks broken, or a link doesn't work

Please tell us directly — this is a small, actively maintained platform, and specific reports get fixed fast.
