Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 July 2026.

Who runs Bridge NG

Bridge NG is an independent project built and operated by one person, not a company with a dedicated legal or privacy team. This policy is written in plain language so you know exactly what happens to your information.

What we collect

When you create an account, we store what you give us on signup and in your profile: your name, email, date of birth, sex, phone number, address, education, career stage, field of study, preferred location, remote-work preference, and skills. If you use other features, we also store the job applications you submit, saved searches, companies you follow, and appointment requests you make. Employers who post a role provide the job details they enter.

Passwords

Your password is never stored in plain text. It's hashed with PBKDF2 (200,000 iterations) and a unique random salt per account before it ever touches the database — not even the person running this site can see your actual password.

Where your data lives

Account and profile data is stored in a database (SQLite or Turso, depending on deployment) that only the site operator can access directly. A session cookie (bridge_session) is used solely to keep you signed in — it is not used for tracking or advertising.

Third-party services

This site loads a few third-party scripts that may set their own cookies or collect data under their own privacy policies, outside Bridge NG's control:

  • Google Fonts (for typography)
  • jsPDF and docx (used only in your browser to generate downloadable files — no data is sent anywhere for this)

What we don't do

We don't sell your data. We don't share your profile with employers except through the matching features you use directly (e.g. an employer sees your profile only if you're shown to them as a match, or if you apply to their posting).

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your data at any time by emailing hannasridwan6006@gmail.com. We'll handle it directly and promptly.

Changes to this policy

As Bridge NG grows, this policy may be updated. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date at the top.
